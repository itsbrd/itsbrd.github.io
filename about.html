Project Poop is an experimental music and web art project created by itsbrd.

This website blends music, interactive elements, and retro computer aesthetics into a single experience. Inspired by old CRT monitors, terminal interfaces, and strange corners of the early internet.

Use the terminal on the home page to explore hidden commands and discover secrets scattered throughout the site.

Check back regularly for updates, and add me on Discord if you have any questions or suggestions!
